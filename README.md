# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using Excel to identify workforce groups with higher attrition rates and provide data-driven insights into employee retention.

## Business Objective
The objective is to understand how employee attrition varies across departments, age groups, job roles, tenure, overtime, job satisfaction, monthly income, and business travel.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- PivotTables – Data summarization and analysis
- PivotCharts and Excel Charts – Data visualization
- Excel formulas – KPI calculations
- Interactive dashboard design

## Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Employees Who Left | 237 |
| Active Employees | 1,233 |
| Overall Attrition Rate | 16.12% |

## Key Insights
1. Employees under 25 have the highest age-group attrition rate at 39.18%.
2. Employees working overtime have a 30.53% attrition rate, compared with 10.44% for employees not working overtime.
3. Employees with less than two years at the company have a 34.88% attrition rate.
4. Sales Representatives have the highest job-role attrition rate at 39.76%.
5. Employees earning below 3,000 monthly have the highest income-group attrition rate at 28.61%.

*These findings show associations in the dataset and do not establish the causes of employee attrition.*
